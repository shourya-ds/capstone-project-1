About Us

A small cafe with a big love for food.

Our Story

Tasty Bites started in 2020 as a tiny cafe with just four tables and a simple idea: serve tasty food at prices everyone can afford. What began as a neighbourhood favourite is now loved by thousands of families and students across the city.

We cook everything fresh every single day. Our vegetables come from local farms, our sauces are made in-house, and our chefs never take shortcuts. When you eat at Tasty Bites, you are eating food made the right way.
